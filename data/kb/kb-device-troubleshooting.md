# Basic device troubleshooting

Keywords (UA): не вмикається, не заряджається, гальмує, не підключається, Bluetooth, оновлення прошивки

Before contacting support, try:
1. Charge the device for 30 minutes with the original cable and adapter.
2. Restart: hold the power button for 10–15 seconds.
3. Update the firmware or the companion app (for smart watches, earbuds and robot vacuums).
4. For Bluetooth problems: remove the device from the phone's Bluetooth list and pair again.
5. Factory reset as described in the manual (this deletes your data).

If the problem remains, contact support with the order number and a short description — we will route it to warranty service.
**Safety:** if a battery is swollen, hot or smells of smoke, stop using and charging the device immediately and contact support.
