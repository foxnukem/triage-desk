# Receipts and invoices

Keywords (UA): чек, рахунок, накладна, фіскальний чек, документи для бухгалтерії, ПДВ

An electronic fiscal receipt is emailed automatically after payment. You can also download it from "My orders".
Business customers can request an invoice and a delivery note for accounting: send the order number, company name and EDRPOU code to support. Documents are prepared within 2 business days.
Gadgetko is a VAT payer; VAT invoices are registered in the tax system within the legal deadline.
