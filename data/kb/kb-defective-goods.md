# Defective or damaged on arrival

Keywords (UA): брак, пошкоджений, розбитий екран, тріснув, не вмикається з коробки, дефект

If an item arrived damaged (cracked screen, dents) or does not work out of the box:
1. Report it within **14 days of delivery** with the order number and photos of the item and the packaging.
2. Gadgetko arranges a free pickup by Nova Poshta. Return shipping is free for defective items.
3. After inspection (up to 3 business days) you choose: a **replacement** of the same model, or a **full refund** of the item price.

If the parcel was visibly damaged at the post office, ask Nova Poshta to write a damage report (акт) — it speeds up the process.
After 14 days, defects are handled under the warranty — see "Warranty".
