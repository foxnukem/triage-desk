# Warranty and repairs

Keywords (UA): гарантія, ремонт, сервісний центр, зламався, гарантійний випадок

All devices have at least a **12-month manufacturer warranty**; Apple and Samsung devices are serviced by authorised service centres.
- Within the warranty, bring or send the device with the receipt to a service centre listed on the warranty card, or ask support to arrange pickup.
- Diagnosis takes up to 14 days; repair under warranty is free.
- If a repair is impossible, the device is replaced or refunded according to the law.

The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage or unauthorised repair.
