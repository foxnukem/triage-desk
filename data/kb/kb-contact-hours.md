# Support hours and escalation

Keywords (UA): підтримка, графік роботи, оператор, скарга, менеджер

Support works every day from 08:00 to 21:00 (Kyiv time) by chat, email support@gadgetko.ua and phone 0 800 300 123.
First response time: within 4 hours during working hours. Urgent issues (security, safety, payment failures) are handled first.
If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may ask for escalation to a senior agent. Complaints are reviewed within 3 business days.
