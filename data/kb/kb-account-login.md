# Cannot log in to your account

Keywords (UA): не можу увійти, забув пароль, акаунт заблоковано, код не приходить, вхід

- Forgot password: use "Forgot password" on the login page; the link is valid for 1 hour.
- The account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts.
- SMS code not arriving: check that the phone number is correct and wait 2 minutes before requesting a new code.

If you think someone else accessed your account (unknown orders, changed email), contact support immediately: we block the account and review recent orders. Never share SMS codes with anyone, including people claiming to be Gadgetko staff.
