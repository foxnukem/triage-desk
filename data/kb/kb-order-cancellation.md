# Cancelling or changing an order

Keywords (UA): скасувати замовлення, відмінити, змінити адресу, змінити замовлення

- Before dispatch, an order can be cancelled or changed (address, items) in "My orders" or by contacting support.
- After dispatch, cancellation is not possible; you can refuse the parcel at the Nova Poshta branch, and the refund is issued after it returns to our warehouse.
- Refunds for cancelled orders follow "Refund timing".
