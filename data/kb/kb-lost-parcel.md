# Delayed or lost parcel

Keywords (UA): посилка не прийшла, загубили, затримка доставки, довго їде

If the parcel is more than **3 days late** compared with the estimated date, contact support with your order number. We open an investigation with Nova Poshta (up to 5 business days).
- If the parcel is confirmed lost, we send a **new item at no cost** or give a **full refund**, your choice.
- If it is found, we expedite delivery.

Gadgetko does **not** pay monetary compensation, vouchers or discounts for delivery delays. Shipping fees are refunded only if the parcel is confirmed lost.
