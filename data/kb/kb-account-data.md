# Changing account data and deleting the account

Keywords (UA): змінити пошту, змінити телефон, видалити акаунт, персональні дані, розсилка

- Name, address and phone can be changed in "Profile".
- Changing the email requires confirmation from both the old and the new address. If you lost access to the old email, support verifies your identity by recent order details.
- To stop marketing emails, use the "Unsubscribe" link.
- Account deletion: request it from support; personal data is deleted within 30 days, except data we must keep by law (receipts).
