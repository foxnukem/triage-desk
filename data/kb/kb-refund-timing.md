# Refund timing

Keywords (UA): повернення коштів, коли повернуть гроші, рефанд, терміни

Refunds are always made to the original payment method.
- We issue the refund within **5 business days** after the returned item passes inspection, or after an order is cancelled.
- Bank cards: the money usually appears in **3–10 business days** after we issue it, depending on your bank.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded by bank transfer to an IBAN you provide.
- Installment plans (оплата частинами): the bank cancels the plan; remaining payments stop.

Gadgetko does not pay interest or extra compensation on refunds. Support agents cannot speed up the bank's part of the process.
