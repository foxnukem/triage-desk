# Promo codes and price changes

Keywords (UA): промокод, знижка, акція, ціна знизилась, бонуси

- One promo code per order; it must be entered before payment and cannot be applied afterwards.
- If the price of an item drops after your purchase, Gadgetko does **not** refund the difference. You can return a non-defective item within 14 days and buy it again at the new price.
- Bonus points are credited 14 days after delivery and can pay for up to 20% of a future order.
