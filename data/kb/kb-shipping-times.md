# Shipping options and delivery times

Keywords (UA): доставка, скільки їде, терміни доставки, Нова пошта, кур'єр

- Nova Poshta branch or parcel locker: **1–3 days** across Ukraine, free for orders over 1,500 UAH.
- Nova Poshta courier to your door: 1–3 days, 99 UAH.
- Same-day courier in Kyiv for orders placed before 12:00.

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day after payment. Delivery times may grow during holidays and power outages.
Gadgetko does not ship outside Ukraine.
