# Payment methods

Keywords (UA): оплата, способи оплати, карта, Apple Pay, оплата частинами, накладений платіж

We accept:
- Visa and Mastercard cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay,
- installments (оплата частинами) from monobank and PrivatBank for orders over 2,000 UAH,
- cash on delivery at Nova Poshta (the carrier charges a 2% fee),
- bank transfer for business customers (invoice).

If a payment fails, check the card limit and 3-D Secure confirmation, then try again. If checkout shows an error for all cards, it may be a payment provider outage — please contact support.
