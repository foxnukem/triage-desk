# Tracking an order

Keywords (UA): де моє замовлення, відстежити, ТТН, трекінг, статус замовлення

After dispatch you receive an SMS and an email with the Nova Poshta tracking number (ТТН). You can track the parcel:
- in "My orders" on gadgetko.ua,
- in the Nova Poshta app or website using the ТТН.

To help you, support needs your **order number** in the format GK-XXXXXX. You can find it in the order confirmation email.
If the status has not changed for more than 3 days, see "Delayed or lost parcel".
