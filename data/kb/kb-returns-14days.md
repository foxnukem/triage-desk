# Returning a non-defective item within 14 days

Keywords (UA): повернення, повернути товар, 14 днів, не підійшов, обмін

You can return any item bought at Gadgetko within **14 days of delivery** if it was not used, is complete and is in its original packaging. This follows the Ukrainian consumer protection law.

How to return:
1. Open "My orders" and press "Return", or reply to the support agent with your order number (GK-XXXXXX).
2. Send the parcel back via Nova Poshta to the address in the return form. Return shipping is paid by the customer for non-defective items.
3. After the warehouse inspects the item (up to 2 business days), the refund is issued — see "Refund timing".

Not returnable: opened headphones and earbuds (hygiene), activated software licences and gift cards.
Defective or damaged items are handled differently — see "Defective or damaged on arrival".
