# Charged twice or wrong amount

Keywords (UA): списали двічі, подвійне списання, зайва сума, неправильна сума, заблокували кошти

Sometimes a failed payment attempt leaves a **hold** on your card. Holds are not charges and are released by your bank automatically in **3–7 business days**.
If two charges are actually posted for one order:
1. Send support the order number and a bank statement or screenshot with both charges.
2. The billing team verifies the duplicate within 2 business days.
3. The duplicate amount is refunded to the same card within 5 business days after verification.

Wrong amount charged (price differs from the order) is handled the same way: the difference is refunded after verification.
